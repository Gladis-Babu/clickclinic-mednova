# MedNova Project Brief

## Deliverable
Create a polished English Word brief that presents MedNova as a UAE-focused post-screening follow-up platform. The document will be suitable for judges, sponsors, clinical stakeholders, and presentation use.

## Content structure
1. **The observation** — use the five synthetic test cases to show how different results, missing history, medicine context, and missed confirmation create serious follow-up risks.
2. **The problem, stated properly** — frame the gap between screening and completed follow-up, supported by current UAE statistics from authoritative sources.
3. **Why it is a UAE problem** — connect diabetes risk, population diversity, language needs, preventive campaigns, and continuity of care.
4. **Who is affected** — patients, families, clinicians, reviewers, care coordinators, facilities, and health authorities, with the named synthetic cases as concrete examples.
5. **The solution shape** — explain the rules-based pathway, human review gates, reminders, consultation/location steps, population funnel, clinician workflow, and auditability.
6. **What the prototype proves** — describe the working patient and healthcare-worker views, five shared cases, live engine checks, multilingual support, and consistent traceability.
7. **What it changes** — clearly contrast today’s fragmented follow-up with a safer, visible, accountable pathway.
8. **The patient’s screen** — organized explanation of result, meaning, status, next action, reminders, map, timeline, questions, and privacy-safe notifications.
9. **Keeping the record safe** — distinguish current synthetic/session-only safeguards from production requirements.
10. **Known limitations** — concise, honest points covering prototype data, authentication, integrations, clinical validation, credential verification, storage, notifications, and translation review.
11. **What would come next in the UAE** — propose a practical pilot, governance, integrations, UAE data residency, clinical and security validation, Arabic review, licensing checks, and outcome measurement.

## Evidence and tone
- Use official UAE/MOHAP/DoH sources where available and clearly cite every external statistic.
- Keep claims accurate: MedNova supports follow-up; it does not diagnose, prescribe, or claim live integration.
- Make the writing impactful but credible, with short sections, callouts, and a strong closing statement.
- Clearly label Fatima, Noor, Huda, Rashid, and Sara as synthetic demonstration cases.

## Quality checks
- Verify all case values, rules, and workflows against the project’s shared case data.
- Validate the Word file and visually inspect the rendered pages for readability and layout.
